WHO WE ARE

WE SUPPLY VALUE - WOVEN & NON-WOVEN FUSIBLE INTERLINING

With over 20 years of specialization, we supply value-added, woven & non-woven fusible interlining solutions for the apparel industry. We excel in providing tailored services that encompass every phase: from production and stringent quality assurance to cost efficiency and punctual delivery.

Our extensive product portfolio spans diverse categories, including Warp Knitted Interlining, Circular Interlining, Water Jet Loom Interlining, and Woven Interlining, ensuring comprehensive solutions for varied manufacturing requirements.
