11 / SUSTAINABILITY PLAN

OUR 3 CORE CONCEPTS: REDUCE, REUSE, RESPECT

1. REDUCE

Reducing energy consumption, eliminating paper waste with digital tech packages, and minimizing carbon footprint across manufacturing.

2. REUSE

Recycling process water, utilizing recycled polyester fibers (GRS certified), and reusing production materials efficiently.

3. RESPECT

Respecting environment safety standards, workplace ethics (BSCI audited), and strict compliance with OEKO-TEX 100 harmful substance rules.
